Meet Your Instructor — Drake von Trapp

Drake von Trapp is a belly dance historian, researcher, teacher, and performer recognized for his pioneering research on male belly dancers in the U.S. He holds a BA and MA in Dance and a post-master's in Multicultural Women's and Gender Studies from Texas Woman's University, and is pursuing a PhD in Performance Studies at UT Austin.

His background is an interplay of fusion and American cabaret belly dance, combined with interdisciplinary training in contemporary modern, ballet, and West African dance.

Hafla Al-Anwar

The Festival of Lights

📅 Saturday, September 5, 2026 | 7:00 PM – 11:00 PM

📍 Schoolyard Winery Patio · 14923 Walton-Verona Road · Verona, KY

Fiery Nights & Tribal Temptations Belly Dance proudly presents the first annual HAFLA AL-ANWAR — The Festival of Lights! As Labor Day weekend is in full swing, we gather on the Schoolyard Winery patio to celebrate the ancient art of belly dance with fire, light, and community under the September stars!

Hafla Al-Anwar honors the centuries-old belly dance tradition of fire and candlelight, reimagined under the Kentucky night sky with fire props, dazzling LED performances, glowing candles, and pure belly dance artistry!

  • 🔥 Fire prop performances
  • 💡 LED light performances
  • 🕯️ Candle performances
  • 💃 All styles of belly dance
  • 🔥 Fire pit glowing on the patio
  • ⭐ Dancing under the stars and string lights
  • 🎵 Music all evening
  • 🍽️ Food
  • 🍷 Wine & bourbon bar open all evening!
  • ❤️ Community · Family · Celebration
